## About the Website

This is the official website of **Lantzounato** (Λαντζουνάτο), a small mountain village nestled in the highlands of **Trifylía**, Messenia, Greece. The site is open-source and hosted on **GitHub Pages**, built to share the village's history, landmarks, events, and practical information with visitors and locals alike.

---

## 📍 About the Village

Lantzounato sits at an altitude of **651 metres**, surrounded by the tall mountains of Trifylía. It is located:
- **28 km** from Kyparissia
- **47 km** from Kalamata

In ancient times, the area was part of the **Kingdom of Nestor of Ancient Pylos** — a rich historical legacy that lives on in the landscape and local culture.

---

## 🗂️ What the Website Covers

### 🏛️ Sights & Landmarks
- **Saflaouro Castle** — A Frankish fortress (13th–14th century) perched on a strategic hilltop, offering panoramic views of the Messenian plain and the mountains of Arcadia and Trifylía.
- **Nikoloulia Fountain** — A beautifully crafted stone fountain in an idyllic southern setting, with fresh, light water and local legend tied to its builder.
- **Agios Athanasios (Maï Thanasís)** — A charming church at the foot of Saflaouro Castle, reachable by a short forest trail from the village.
- **Panaghitsa Chapel** — A tiny chapel wedged beneath an impressive rock formation, perfect for a short visit or photography.
- **Lakes of Vagia, Panagiotaki & others** — The Arkadian river forms small waterfalls and crystal-clear lakes with breathtaking natural scenery.
- **Alití Spring** — A natural spring near the site of the abandoned ancient settlement of Analytí.

### 🥾 Hiking Trails
The website lists several walking routes connecting the village square, springs, chapels, and the castle, with more details coming soon.

### 🎉 Events
Upcoming local events include:
- **Panigiri** (village festival)
- **Open-Air Cinema** (summer)

### ☕ Amenities & Services
- A **café** in the village square, shaded by plane trees, offering coffee, refreshments, and **free Wi-Fi**.
- A **24-hour automated local radio station** broadcasting podcasts, daily weather forecasts, and local news — tune in at **108 MHz**.
